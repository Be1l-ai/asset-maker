MORE EXAMPLES, BETTER QUESTIONS

What should we improve next?

Concrete review targets, not a claim of production readiness.

01 / GEOMETRY → GAMEPLAY

Make the props usable, not just viewable.

Observed: the pine is readable but origami-like; the chest has clear plankwork but plain endcaps and no wood grain. The 868 / 2,856-triangle models remain static, overlapping solid assemblies with vertex colors, not game-ready interactive props.

Next experiment: separate the lid with a hinge pivot, add a simple collision proxy, and test both in one target engine. Evaluate materials there rather than trusting the software preview.

02 / ART → ACTUAL DISPLAY SIZE

Judge artwork where players see it.

Observed: the shield silhouette and bright emblem survive at 32 / 64 px, but the rivets and central jewel lose definition. The panel’s separate 768 × 256 nine-slice preview preserves its corner proportions.

Next experiment: compare the shield at 32 / 48 / 64 px on light and dark UI. Simplify secondary details that merge together; give item families a shared outline weight and palette.

03 / EFFECTS → TIMING

Give each effect its own rhythm.

Observed: the dust billows read as rounded lumps more than turbulent air, and lightning’s late tail is nearly invisible on a light background. Both still share a 16-frame, one-second delivery format despite needing different timing.

Next experiment: break up the dust silhouette with coherent turbulence, then compare faster lightning and slower dust decay. Choose frame count and playback rate per effect; check opacity on light and dark backgrounds.

04 / SOUND → REPETITION

Evaluate a sequence, not one sound.

Observed: the pack adds a noisy footstep and a charge/release shape beyond the original tonal examples. They are still isolated mono cues; waveform, headroom, and playback tests cannot establish convincing timbre.

Next experiment: render several footstep seeds, listen at matched levels, and test a walk/run sequence. Adjust heel-to-grit balance and variation before adding reverb. Human listening is still pending.

Recommendation: build one small, coherent interaction next—a chest opening with an item icon, dust, and sound—and review it in the target engine. The current libraries already cover the fundamentals; coordinated art direction, timing, and engine checks are more valuable now than another library or dozens of unrelated presets. Original exports remain unchanged.
